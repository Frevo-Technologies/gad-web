## Public notice

We, M/s
G.A. Digital Web Word Pvt. Ltd.
, hereby publish in the interest of the general public that our Company is engaged in the providing Outsourced Manpower services to the Government entities on contractual basis for the fixed/certain period of 12 months as mandated under contractual obligations. That in pursuance of the on-going contract executed with the Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DESUP), our Company is obligated to provide outsourced manpower to the Government entity in accordance with the fair and reasonable recruitment and placement process. It is stated in the above-mentioned context that we do not engage in/or endorse any unlawful money transactions, including taking any money for favored placement of the candidate in the Government entity. We have no role in accepting any money or offering any form of inducement for employment. It has been brought to our notice that touts have taken money from potential candidates for securing favored placement in above-stated recruitment/selection process. It is unequivocally stated that our Company has no association with any outside agency or brokers working in their own individual capacity. It is further stated that our Company does not charge any fee for job placement. If any candidate is found to have paid any money to any person for securing a job opportunity in the above stated selection drive, their candidature shall be rejected immediately for indulging into misconduct and no further requests for reconsideration shall be entertained in this regard. Furthermore, it is important to mention herein that candidates shall participate in the placement process with honesty and sincerity. Any person found using fake/fraudulent means to secure placement, including submitting fake experience certificates, shall be subject to stringent legal action against them and their candidature shall be cancelled forthwith.
Any person/tout found misusing our name for any of such above-stated purposes is stated to have acted independently and unlawfully without any association with our Company and its vision and shall face stringent legal action against them. Kindly report such instances to us immediately for assisting us for taking appropriate legal actions against such touts.
(Ms. Riya Sethi) Legal Advisor
For G.A. Digital Web Word Pvt. Ltd.
